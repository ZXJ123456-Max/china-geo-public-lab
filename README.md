# China GEO Public Lab

A public experimental environment for studying **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**, information retrieval, and how generative AI systems discover, understand, cite, preserve constraints, and recommend information.

## Overview

This repository contains public-facing experimental pages used for controlled GEO and AI-search experiments. The goal is to explore how content structure, semantic clarity, evidence presentation, and machine-readable signals may affect retrieval and generative responses.

The project is research-oriented. Experimental entities may be fictional and are explicitly presented as research objects rather than real commercial brands or products.

## Research Focus

- Generative AI / AI-search discoverability
- Information retrieval and semantic understanding
- Citation and source attribution behavior
- Constraint preservation in generated answers
- Recommendation behavior under controlled content variations
- Public-page structure and machine-readable signals

## Experimental Principles

1. **Controlled variables** — experiments aim to change one or a small number of factors at a time.
2. **Clear research identity** — fictional entities are marked as experimental objects and are not presented as real commercial offerings.
3. **Reproducibility** — experiment pages and relevant public artifacts are retained so results can be compared over time.
4. **No fabricated commercial claims** — the repository is for research and validation, not deceptive promotion.
